airpick Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Launch date], 2026

airpick ("Service") respects user privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, stored, and deleted.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Service does not require account registration or login. We do not collect:

  • Name, date of birth, gender
  • Email, phone number (except when voluntarily provided in support inquiries)
  • Physical address or location data
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers (Google AAID, Apple IDFA)
  • Third-party analytics or behavioral tracking data

2. Automatically Collected Information

3. User-Provided Settings

The following settings are stored alongside the push token:

All such settings are non-personally-identifiable values used solely to deliver tailored notifications.

4. Purpose of Use

  • Sending flight-deal push notifications
  • Service operation and error response
  • Responding to user inquiries

We do not use information for marketing or advertising purposes, and we do not sell user data to third parties.

8. User Rights

  • Opt out of notifications: Toggle off the in-app alarm or block notifications in device settings
  • Data deletion: Uninstalling the app automatically deactivates the push token. For immediate complete deletion, contact us by email; we will process within 7 business days.
  • Access and correction: We do not collect identifying data, but you may request information about your push token data by email.
  • EU/UK residents additionally have rights under GDPR including data portability, restriction, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. Advertising and Revenue Model

  • No in-app advertising
  • No advertising identifiers collected
  • Service is supported by affiliate commissions when users book flights through the linked airlines/OTAs
  • Clicking affiliate links redirects to external sites governed by their own privacy policies

10. Children Under 14

The Service is not intended for children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age.

11. Security Measures

  • All communications encrypted via HTTPS (TLS)
  • Database access controlled by Row Level Security (RLS) policies
  • Administrative keys stored in secure secret vaults
  • Access logs retained and reviewed regularly
